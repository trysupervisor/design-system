Device frame image sources

The Device component and its measured frame coordinates are distributed
under the Supervisor MIT license. The preset PNG images are hosted by
Mobile FIRST and are not included in this package or covered by that license.

Mobile FIRST permits personal and commercial use, modification, and cropping
of these mockups. Its model pages prohibit resale or redistribution of the
frame file alone. Refer to the source pages for the image terms before
downloading, hosting, or otherwise using the files.

The component requests the original image URLs directly. It does not proxy,
cache, or optimize them. Their availability depends on the provider. Set
frameSrc to a local path to use your own authorized copy of a preset PNG,
or supply a custom frame with its own image and screen coordinates.
