MacBook Pro 14 inch

public/devices/macbook-pro-14-space-black.png is a retouched adaptation of
AzureSaturn's photograph of a 14 inch M5 MacBook Pro in Space Black.
The source photograph was published under CC0. Supervisor releases this
adaptation under CC0 as well, to the extent it holds rights in the image.

Source photograph:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MacBook_Pro_(14-inch,_M5,_Space_Black).jpg
License:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Changes: reconstructed a centered front view with an image editing tool,
removed the surroundings and screen content, and added an alpha channel.
This is a retouched illustration based on a photograph, not an unaltered
product photograph or an official Apple asset.

Editing brief: preserve the slim bezel, camera notch, dark aluminum body,
and metallic edges; use a straight front view with a narrow base; remove
all surrounding objects and screen content; create a transparent PNG frame.

The PNG is served from:
https://ui.trysupervisor.com/devices/macbook-pro-14-space-black.png
